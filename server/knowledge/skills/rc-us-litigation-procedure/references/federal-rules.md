# Federal procedure reference  (as of 2026-10)

Rule texts read 2026-10-10 (law.cornell.edu as a finder; uscourts.gov for what is in force). Verify against the current official
rules and the district's local rules before relying on any line here.

## FRCP essentials
- **Rule 4 service**: server 18+ and not a party; waiver request gives 30 days (60 abroad) to return and then 60/90 days to answer;
  refusing waiver without good cause shifts service costs (4(d)(2)). Individuals: state-law method or personal / abode / agent
  (4(e)). Entities: officer, managing or general agent, or agent by appointment (4(h)). United States: US attorney plus certified
  mail to the Attorney General, plus the agency/officer (4(i)). Proof by server's affidavit (4(l)); 90-day limit (4(m)).
- **Rule 5**: e-service through CM/ECF on registered users is complete on filing, not effective if the sender learns it failed
  (5(b)(2)(E)); no certificate needed for CM/ECF service (5(d)(1)(B)). Represented parties must e-file; unrepresented parties
  only if an order or local rule allows (5(d)(3)). Clerk may not refuse a paper only for form (5(d)(4)).
- **Rule 5.2 privacy**: last 4 of SSN/TIN and financial accounts, birth year, minor initials; sealed filing by order; waiver by own filing.
- **Rule 6**: see `deadlines.md`. 6(b): extensions for good cause; after expiry needs a motion and excusable neglect; no extension of
  50(b), (d), 52(b), 59(b), (d), (e), 60(b).
- **Rule 7(b)**: motion in writing, particular grounds, relief sought. **Rule 7.1** disclosure statements (corporate and, in diversity
  cases, citizenship of every party).
- **Rule 8**: short and plain statement; affirmative defenses listed in 8(c); denials must fairly respond.
- **Rule 11**: signature (pro se signs personally, with address, email, phone); certifies proper purpose, legal support, factual
  support, warranted denials; 21-day safe harbor on a separate motion; court may act on its own; sanction limited to deterrence;
  no monetary sanction on a represented party for 11(b)(2); does not apply to discovery (26-37).
- **Rule 12**: answer 21 days; defenses 12(b)(1)-(7); 12(g)/(h) consolidation and waiver (personal jurisdiction, venue, process,
  service waived if omitted); subject-matter jurisdiction any time; 12(d) converts to summary judgment if outside matters are used.
- **Rule 15**: amend once as of course within 21 days; otherwise consent or leave "freely" given.
- **Rule 16.1** (new 2025): initial MDL management conference and report.
- **Rule 26(f)/16(b)** (2025): discovery plan and scheduling order address timing and method for privilege logs under 26(b)(5)(A).
- **Rule 41**: voluntary dismissal by notice before the opposing party serves an answer or summary-judgment motion, or by stipulation
  of all appearing parties (41(a)(1)(A)); a notice dismissal is an adjudication on the merits if the plaintiff previously dismissed
  any federal or state action on the same claim (41(a)(1)(B), two-dismissal rule).
- **Rule 54(b)**: final judgment on fewer than all claims/parties only with an express "no just reason for delay" determination.
- **Rule 55**: clerk enters default on affidavit (55(a)); clerk judgment only for sum certain against a non-appearing, competent
  adult (55(b)(1)); otherwise court, with 7 days' notice to a party who appeared (55(b)(2)); set aside entry for good cause, judgment
  under 60(b) (55(c)); against the US only on satisfactory evidence (55(d)).
- **Rule 56**: standard 56(a); timing 56(b); record citations 56(c)(1); admissibility objection 56(c)(2); affidavits/declarations
  on personal knowledge 56(c)(4); 56(d) more discovery; 56(e) unsupported facts; 56(h) bad-faith affidavits sanctioned.
- **Rule 58**: separate document; FRAP 4(a)(7) deems entry 150 days after docketing if no separate document.
- **Rule 60**: (a) clerical errors any time (leave of appellate court once appeal docketed); (b)(1)-(6); (c)(1) timing; (c)(2) no
  suspension of the judgment; (d) independent action and fraud on the court preserved.
- **Rule 62**: 30-day automatic stay; stay by bond or other security any time after judgment; injunction judgments not stayed
  unless ordered; injunction pending appeal under 62(d).
- **Rule 65**: PI only on notice; TRO without notice needs sworn specific facts of immediate irreparable harm plus the movant's
  attorney certification of notice efforts; 14-day maximum, extendable for good cause or by consent; motion to dissolve on 2 days'
  notice; security (US exempt); orders state reasons and specific terms, bind parties and those in active concert with actual notice.

## FRAP essentials
- **FRAP 3**: notice of appeal filed with the district clerk within the Rule 4 time (3(a)(1)); names each appealing party, designates
  the judgment or appealable order, names the court appealed to (3(c)(1)); the notice encompasses all orders that merge into the
  designated judgment (3(c)(4)); designating a final order or a Rule 4(a)(4)(A) order encompasses the final judgment (3(c)(5)); a
  notice limits the appeal only by an express statement (3(c)(6)).
- **FRAP 4**: see `deadlines.md`. Inmate filings timely if deposited in the institution mail system with a declaration (4(c)).
- **FRAP 8**: ask the district court first for a stay, bond approval, or injunction pending appeal; in the court of appeals show it
  was impracticable or denied, give reasons and sworn facts, attach record parts, notify all parties; bond may be required.

## FRE essentials
- 103 (preserve: timely objection with specific ground; offer of proof), 104 (preliminary questions), 201 (judicial notice: not
  reasonably disputed), 401-403, 801-807 hearsay, 901-902 authentication (902(11)-(12) certified business records with notice;
  902(13)-(14) machine-generated records and copied device data, certified), 1001-1008 originals, 1006 summaries (2024: admissible
  whether or not the underlying records are in evidence), 107 illustrative aids (2024: not evidence).
- **801(d)(1)(A) after 2026-12-01**: any prior inconsistent statement of a testifying witness subject to cross is not hearsay.

## Jurisdiction and venue (Title 28)
- 1331 federal question; 1332(a) diversity: complete diversity and a matter in controversy exceeding $75,000 exclusive of interest and
  costs (text read 2026-10-10; last amended 2011); 1367 supplemental;
  1391 venue; 1404/1406 transfer; 1441 removal; 1446 procedure (30 days; all served defendants consent; 1-year diversity cap
  unless bad faith; notice to state court stops it); 1447 remand; 1654 self-representation; 1738 full faith and credit; 1746
  declarations; 1927 counsel liability for vexatious multiplication; 2107 appeal times (statute behind FRAP 4).

## E-filing, local rules, standing orders
- CM/ECF (and NextGen) per district; PACER for dockets. Find each court through uscourts.gov "Court Website Links".
- Read: local civil rules, the judge's standing orders and "practices" page, ECF administrative procedures, sealed-filing orders
  (several districts changed after the 2025 cyberattacks: e.g., M.D.N.C. notice of 2025-08-15, no sealed documents through CM/ECF
  from 2025-08-20, paper only; E.D. Tenn. SO-25-12 and D.N.D. 2025-09-24: sealed filings cannot be served through CM/ECF, serve by
  other means), pro se handbook.
- AI: N.D. Tex. LR 7.2(f) (effective 2024-09-03) disclosure on first page of any brief prepared with generative AI; individual judges
  in many districts require certification that citations were checked by a human. Search the judge's page for "artificial intelligence".

## Pro se and entities
- 28 U.S.C. 1654: individuals may proceed personally. Entities (corporations, LLCs, partnerships, associations) appear only through
  licensed counsel (*Rowland v. California Men's Colony*, 506 U.S. 194 (1993), verified 2026-10-10). A non-lawyer cannot represent
  another person, including a family member (general rule; check circuit law).
- Many districts have pro se clinics and handbooks; courts read pro se filings liberally but still enforce deadlines.
