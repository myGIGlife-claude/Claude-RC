# Document structure templates  (as of 2026-10)

Skeletons only. Every `[BRACKET]` is a blank the person fills from real records. Never invent facts, case numbers, dates,
signatures, notary blocks or service details. Replace these generic headings with the court's required caption, format, font,
margins and page limits (local rules, judge's standing orders). Remove all bracket text before filing; a leftover bracket is a defect.

## 1. Motion
```
[COURT NAME exactly as on prior filings]
[COUNTY / DIVISION / DISTRICT]

[PARTY NAME], [Plaintiff/Petitioner],          Case No. [FROM DOCKET]
    v.                                           Judge [NAME, if the court requires]
[PARTY NAME], [Defendant/Respondent].           Magistrate [NAME, if any]

[MOVING PARTY]'S MOTION FOR [SPECIFIC RELIEF]

[Moving party] moves the Court under [rule/statute, e.g., Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(b)(6) / Ohio Civ.R. 60(B) / N.C. R. Civ. P. 60(b)]
for an order [exact relief, one sentence]. The grounds are: [2-4 short numbered grounds, each tied to a fact source].
[Required statements: conference with opposing counsel / consent sought (if a local rule requires), oral argument requested?]
A memorandum in support [and proposed order] [is/are] attached.

Respectfully submitted,
[SIGNATURE BLOCK LEFT UNSIGNED: name, bar number or "self-represented", address, phone, email as the rule requires]
[Date: LEFT BLANK FOR SIGNER]

CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE  [COMPLETE ONLY AFTER SERVICE ACTUALLY HAPPENS]
I certify that on [DATE OF ACTUAL SERVICE] I served a copy of this [document] on [NAME(S)] by [METHOD and the rule subdivision,
e.g., Civ.R. 5(B)(2)(c) mail / CM/ECF / File & Serve] at [ADDRESS OR EMAIL OF RECORD].
[SIGNATURE: by the person who served]
```

## 2. Memorandum in support
```
MEMORANDUM IN SUPPORT
I.   Introduction - what the motion asks for and the one-paragraph reason.
II.  Facts - numbered, each with a record cite: (Ex. [X] at [page]) / (Dkt. [no.]) / (Tr. [page]). Allegations labeled as such.
III. Procedural history - only what matters for this motion (dates from the docket).
IV.  Standard - the governing rule and standard from verified authority, with the standard of review if relevant.
V.   Argument - one heading per point, each heading a full-sentence conclusion; element by element; address the strongest
     counterargument and any binding adverse authority honestly.
VI.  Relief requested - exactly what the proposed order says; nothing broader than the court can grant.
[Signature block left unsigned]   [Certificate of word count or page limit, if required]
[Table of contents / authorities if the length or rule requires]
```
Citation audit attached for the person (not filed): each authority, where it was read, date, proposition, still good law?

## 3. Proposed order
```
[CAPTION]
ORDER [GRANTING/DENYING] [MOTION]
This matter is before the Court on [party]'s motion for [relief] (Dkt./filed [date]). Having considered [the motion, response,
record, and argument], the Court [ORDERS]:
1. [Specific, enforceable command; who must do what by when.]
2. [For injunctions: reasons, specific terms, acts described in reasonable detail, bond amount if required (FRCP 65(c)-(d) style).]
[Judge signature line - BLANK]   [Date - BLANK]   [Ohio: final-order language / Civ.R. 54(B) language only if accurate]
```
Never draft a judge's signature, stamp or journal-entry date.

## 4. Affidavit or declaration DRAFT (for the declarant to correct, then sign properly)
```
[CAPTION]
[AFFIDAVIT / DECLARATION] OF [DECLARANT NAME]
1. I am [age 18+ / role] and competent to testify. I have personal knowledge of the facts below [or: state the basis].
2. [One fact per paragraph, in the declarant's own words; dates, places, documents. No legal argument, no conclusions.]
3. Attached as Exhibit [A] is a true copy of [document], which I [created / received / keep in the regular course of ...].
[Choose ONE ending that matches the jurisdiction and filing method:]
- Federal (28 U.S.C. 1746, signed in the US): declaration under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct,
  executed on [DATE - BLANK], signature [BLANK]. Signed abroad: add "under the laws of the United States of America".
- North Carolina (G.S. 7A-98, e-filed matters only): declaration under penalty of perjury under the laws of North Carolina,
  executed on [DATE - BLANK], signature [BLANK]. Otherwise: affidavit sworn before a notary.
- Ohio: affidavit sworn before a notary or other authorized officer [JURAT LEFT FOR THE OFFICER TO COMPLETE].
```
Rules: personal knowledge, admissible facts, competence (FRCP 56(c)(4); Ohio Civ.R. 56(E); NC Rule 56(e)). Tell the declarant to
change anything that is not exactly true; a false sworn statement is perjury. Never notarize, date or sign for anyone.

## 5. Notice of appeal
```
[TRIAL COURT CAPTION - filed in the TRIAL court: federal district court clerk / Ohio trial-court clerk / NC clerk of superior court]
NOTICE OF APPEAL
[Name of each appealing party] appeal[s] to the [Court of Appeals for the ___ Circuit / Court of Appeals of Ohio, ___ Appellate
District / North Carolina Court of Appeals] from the [judgment/order] entered on [DATE OF ENTRY FROM DOCKET] [describe: e.g.,
final judgment granting summary judgment].
[Ohio: attach a time-stamped copy of the judgment (App.R. 3(A)); docketing statement if the local rule requires.]
[Federal: name the judgment; FRAP 3(c). Fee or IFP motion.]
[NC: designate the judgment and the court appealed to; signed by counsel or the unrepresented party (App. R. 3(d)).]
[Signature block - UNSIGNED]   [Certificate of service - complete after service; NC requires serving all parties]
```
Before filing: deadline recomputed from the entry date (and tolling motions) per `deadlines.md`; confirm the order is final or
appealable (54(b) / R.C. 2505.02 / G.S. 1-277); order transcripts on time (Ohio App.R. 9(B); NC App. R. 7 within 14 days).

## 7. Hand-off checklist for the person (always attach)
- What to verify (facts, dates, addresses), what to sign and how (notary or declaration), how and on whom to serve, where and how to
  file (e-filing portal, paper, fees), the deadline with its rule and status, local-rule items still open, and the suggestion to
  consult a licensed lawyer in that jurisdiction for strategy or high-stakes decisions.
