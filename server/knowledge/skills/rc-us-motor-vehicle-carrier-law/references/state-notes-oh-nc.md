## FMCSR adoption for intrastate
- Ohio: OAC 4901:2-5-03 adopts 49 CFR 40, 367, 380, 382, 383, 385, 386, 387, 390-397 (and hazmat parts) as effective 2025-08-01
  (OAC 4901:2-5-02(C), rule eff. 2026-01-01). CMV for intrastate: 390.5 definition for for-hire carriers, 383.5 definition for private
  carriers (OAC 4901:2-5-01(B)). Intrastate construction-materials carriers have special hours (OAC 4901:2-5-03(H): 12 h driving after
  10 off, no driving after 16 h on duty, 70/7 or 80/8).
- NC: State Highway Patrol enforces federal safety and hazmat regulations (G.S. 20-381); "federal safety and hazardous materials
  regulations" = 49 CFR 171-180, 382, 390-398 (G.S. 20-376(1)); intrastate motor carrier = 26,001+ lb, 16+ passengers or placarded hazmat
  (G.S. 20-376(5)). 14B NCAC 07C .0101 (readopted eff. 2018-11-01; read on reports.oah.state.nc.us 2026-10-10): Parts 390-397 apply to
  interstate carriers per 390.5 and to intrastate carriers at 26,001+ lb / 16+ passengers / placarded hazmat; intrastate HOS is 12 h
  driving after 8 off, no driving after 16 h on duty, 70/7 or 80/8; a DMV Exemption Review Officer can waive 391.11(b)(1) age and
  391.41(b)(1)-(11) medical standards intrastate (up to 2 years); **396.17-396.23 periodic inspection applies intrastate from 10,001 lb
  GVWR** with the inspection decal displayed; CVSA out-of-service fines are assessed against the carrier (G.S. 20-17.7).
- USDOT number for intrastate carriers: FMCSA lists both Ohio and North Carolina as states requiring it (fmcsa.dot.gov
  "Do I need a USDOT number", updated 2025-09-03, read via Wayback 2026-10-10); PUCO adopts 390.19T/390.21T for Ohio intrastate
  carriers through OAC 4901:2-5-03(A)-(B). Confirm the state threshold with the agency.

## Title, liens, salvage
- Ohio: a security interest in a motor vehicle is valid against creditors and later purchasers when the clerk notes it on the title or in
  the automated title system (R.C. 4505.13(B)); Chapter 1309 filing does not apply except for dealer inventory (4505.13(A)).
  Dismantled/destroyed vehicles: surrender title to the clerk (4505.11(A)); salvage title rules in 4505.11 (read the section for
  insurer total-loss and rebuilt-inspection steps).
- NC: security interest perfected by notation through DMV (G.S. 20-58 et seq.); perfected as of the agreement date if the application
  and fee reach the Division within 20 days, otherwise on delivery (G.S. 20-58.2). Branded titles: salvage, salvage rebuilt,
  reconstructed, flood, non-USA; insurer total loss marked "TOTAL LOSS CLAIM" (G.S. 20-71.3). "Salvage motor vehicle" = repair cost
  to make it road-safe exceeds 75 % of fair retail market value (NADA or Commissioner-approved guide), total-loss declaration not
  required (G.S. 20-4.01(33)); vehicles up to six model years old retitled after collision damage get DMV pre/final inspections (20-71.3(b)).

## Insurance and financial responsibility
- Ohio: owner's policy minimum 25/50/25 (R.C. 4509.51, eff. 2013-12-22).
- NC: 50/100/50 for policies issued or renewed on/after 2025-07-01 (G.S. 20-279.21(b)(2), S.L. 2023-133 s. 12; S.L. 2024-29 s. 8(c)
  moved the date from 2025-01-01 to 2025-07-01). Before: 30/60/25. Continuous coverage is a registration condition (G.S. 20-309(a)).
- Commercial/for-hire: federal Part 387 levels for interstate; PUCO adopts Part 387 for Ohio intrastate (OAC 4901:2-5-03(A)); NC: the
  owner of a CDL-class CMV (G.S. 20-4.01(3d): Class A 26,001+ lb combination with 10,001+ lb towed unit, Class B, Class C 16+ seats or
  placarded) must carry the 387.9 for-hire nonhazardous property amount, i.e. $750,000 (G.S. 20-309(a1)); other NC intrastate
  for-hire levels are set by the Utilities Commission for certificated carriers (not read: unverified).

## Licence suspension and points (outline)
- Ohio: warning letter above 5 points (R.C. 4510.037(A)); 12 or more points within a two-year period starting at the first conviction
  triggers the suspension notice (4510.037(B)); point schedule in 4510.036. Reinstatement: BMV requirements per suspension
  type (fees, remedial course, proof of insurance).
- NC: suspension for 12 or more points in 3 years, or 8 or more in the 3 years after a reinstatement (G.S. 20-16(a)(5)); speeding
  combinations in 20-16(a)(9). Hearings before DMV hearing officers; points vs insurance points (Safe Driver Incentive Plan) are separate
  systems (unverified detail).
- CDL holders: federal disqualifications (49 CFR 383.51) apply on top; states may not mask, defer or divert a CLP/CDL holder's
  traffic conviction (in any vehicle) off the CDLIS record (49 CFR 384.226, verified 2026-10-10), so PJCs and diversion do not work
  for CDL holders.

## Citations: how to contest (outline, verify local rules)
- Ohio: Ohio Traffic Rules (Traf.R.) govern; municipal, county and mayor's courts; plea options guilty / no contest / not guilty; request
  discovery; a no-contest plea is not an admission in later civil suits (Evid.R. 410, unverified). Points attach on conviction.
- NC: district court; infractions vs misdemeanors; "prayer for judgment continued" (PJC) has limited use for insurance and DMV purposes
  (unverified detail). Many offenses payable online via NC Courts; contest by appearing on the court date.
- Never tell a user a ticket will be dismissed; give the procedure, deadlines from the citation, and the court's contact.

## Lemon laws
- Ohio (R.C. 1345.71-1345.78): covers passenger cars and noncommercial motor vehicles (not mobile homes/RVs living areas); report within
  1 year or 18,000 miles, whichever first; presumption after 3 repairs of the same nonconformity, 30+ days out of service, 8+ total
  attempts, or 1 attempt on a defect likely to cause death or serious injury (1345.73); refund or replacement (1345.72); civil action
  within 5 years of delivery, attorney fees available, limitations tolled during informal dispute resolution (1345.75); manufacturer's
  informal dispute mechanism under 1345.77.
- NC (G.S. 20-351 et seq.): excludes vehicles over 10,000 lb and house trailers (20-351.1(3)); presumption after 4 repairs of the same
  nonconformity or 20+ business days out of service in any 12-month warranty period, with written notice to the manufacturer and up to
  15 days to cure (20-351.5); 10 days' written notice before suing; manufacturer may require an informal procedure that complies with
  Magnuson-Moss if disclosed (20-351.7).

## Towing and storage liens
- Ohio: private tow-away and storage rules in R.C. 4513.60 et seq. (4513.60 eff. 2025-11-25); PUCO sets maximum rates for towing,
  storage and retrieval of vehicles towed under 4513.60/4513.601 (OAC Chapter 4901:2-24, eff. 2018-06-08; definitions in 4901:2-24-01).
- NC: lien for repair, servicing, towing, storage and substitute vehicle rental, with priority over perfected security interests
  (G.S. 44A-2(d)); enforcement by sale under 44A-4 (notice steps); post-towing procedures and hearing rights in G.S. 20-219.9 to
  20-219.14 (Article 7A).
